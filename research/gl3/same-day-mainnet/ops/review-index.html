What this creates

Official Nitro GL3, proposed chain ID 4663001, native GH0ST gas and a Robinhood bridge. No minimum assertion waiting period. Withdrawals become claimable after GH0ST's validator posts and confirms their root; processing and Robinhood fees still apply.

Project-run fast confirmation. ZK verification is not live. The confirmer can approve withdrawal roots and the cold parent administrator can upgrade bridge contracts. This launch depends on those project keys and their availability. It is not a permissionless or instantly guaranteed exit.

The proposed child starts with no allocated balances. A temporary cold setup owner calibrates fees and treasury recipients, then removes its authority before public deposits are enabled. Final owner and native-minter state must be verified; later child configuration changes require a separately reviewed migration or upgrade.
