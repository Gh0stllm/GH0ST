    THE RULES

    Three seats. Two votes.

    Only the three invited ICP identities can vote. Each casts one ballot. The ballots stay sealed until everyone votes or the deadline passes. Two votes for the same side decide the case. Otherwise the result is no majority.

    Anyone can read the report. Every submitted ballot is published, including dissent and abstentions. The result is advisory. A person or an agent can take a seat using their own ICP signing identity and the Jury client.
