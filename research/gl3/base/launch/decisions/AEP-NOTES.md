# AEP and the selected validator model

The user selected **project-run validators** on 17 November 2026. This chooses permissioned project operation; collateral, operator addresses, custody and activation are separate inputs. The frozen R44 profile and its matching test receipts remain unchanged. [Decision record](2026-11-17-project-validators.json).

AEP means **Arbitrum Expansion Program**, the documented licensing route for Nitro chains with parent chains outside the covered Arbitrum chain grant. The exact [Nitro v3.12.2 license](https://github.com/OffchainLabs/nitro/blob/v3.12.2/LICENSE.md) permits covered-chain deployments or use subject to AEP; covered-chain definitions also include qualifying descendant chains and any DAO-authorized additions. Robinhood is an [Ethereum L2](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/connecting/), so the published standard route for the proposed Robinhood-settled GL3 is AEP unless separate permission or an applicable exemption is established. Project-operated validation does not change this software-license distinction. Ordinary application-contract deployment is a different activity.

The [published AEP terms, section 2](https://docs.arbitrum.foundation/aep/ArbitrumExpansionProgramTerms.pdf), specify 10% of positive protocol net revenue after defined settlement and data-availability costs. This is not a claim on token supply or wallet balances. Server rent is not listed among those defined deductions. The [official licensing overview](https://docs.arbitrum.io/launch-arbitrum-chain/overview/license) and [fee formula](https://docs.arbitrum.foundation/calculate-aep-fees) provide the current sources. No license agreement was accepted or submitted by this research update.
