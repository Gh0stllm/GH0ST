GH0ST RESEARCH MODIFICATIONS

The base FP32 checkpoint and its configuration are retained unchanged.
GH0ST added a separately trained 6,915-parameter classification head and
train-split preprocessing statistics. The head was trained only on the
explicitly identified synthetic dataset generated by model_experiment.py.
GH0ST also produced a modified storage representation of the base and head:
symmetric per-tensor signed int8 values with float32 scale factors. Evaluation
of this representation restores float32 weights; it is not native integer
inference. These modified artifacts are in adapted/ and their provenance is
recorded by the adjacent research reports.

The upstream checkpoint, architecture and pretraining are not GH0ST originals.
The synthetic experiment does not establish financial trading performance.
