SMALL, ON PURPOSE

A building block.
Not a general intelligence.

Forge learns categories from a small numeric table. Think NPC behavior, simple routing, and experiments with transparent rules. It does not train chatbots or support arbitrary model architectures. Held-out accuracy describes your dataset’s test split; it is not a promise about the world.
