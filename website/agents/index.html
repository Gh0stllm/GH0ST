What the signature proves

The result is signed by GH0ST’s configured ICP key and bound to your exact request, model and artifact. It authenticates the result; it does not independently prove every model operation. The route also depends on the coordinator, its controllers and chain RPC providers.

The original GH0ST token and native contracts remain on Robinhood. A separate BNB token represents GH0ST locked in the bridge escrow. Use the bridge page to move between chains, then pay for requests here.
