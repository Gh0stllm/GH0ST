05 / BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A little
clarity.

What makes each Genesis unique?+

There are 11 expressions, 9 accent colors and 5 signal placements. Every combination is used once, giving 495 distinct artworks. Each piece is also associated with a different original model-weight shard, including its address, runtime hash and exact model byte range.

Does the protocol take any of the mint tokens?+

No. Every successful mint burns exactly 200,000 GH0ST through the token’s native burn function. The NFT contract retains none of the mint tokens. A separate 3.5% creator royalty is declared for secondary sales. Network gas and marketplace fees are separate.

What is the 3.5% secondary-sale royalty?+

The NFT contract declares a fixed 3.5% creator royalty through ERC-2981. Supporting marketplaces can use it to calculate the royalty when an NFT is resold. ERC-2981 does not force every marketplace or private sale to pay it. The royalty is separate from the 200,000 GH0ST mint burn, and ordinary wallet transfers are not charged by this contract.

How does the three-per-wallet limit work?+

Each address can successfully mint three NFTs over its lifetime. Transferring an NFT away does not reset that count. The limit applies to primary mints, so a wallet may receive more than three NFTs through transfers. It is an address limit, not a limit per person.

What do I own when I mint?+

You own an ERC-721 collectible associated with an original Genesis weight shard. NFT ownership does not transfer model copyright, control over the shard contracts, exclusive inference access or automatic revenue rights.

When can I mint?+

Public minting is live on Robinhood Chain (4663). Connect your wallet, approve 200,000 GH0ST, then burn to mint a random available artwork. Each wallet can mint up to three originals. The page verifies the deployed contract before enabling transactions. Assignment uses secure browser randomness; the deployed contract itself accepts token IDs and does not enforce randomness.

What if another wallet mints the assigned artwork first?+

Each token ID can be minted once. Availability is checked before a mint request, but another transaction can complete first. In that case, your mint reverts and no GH0ST is burned; network gas can still be spent. Try again to receive a new random assignment. No retry transaction is sent automatically.
