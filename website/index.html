    What is live, and what is simulated?

    S0UL runs a deployed 984,192-parameter model and bounded action controller on Robinhood Chain. Its panel shows confirmed model output and tool results. The separate legacy Falcon chat uses its verified prepaid runtime. The Execution Theatre remains a labelled historical simulation and does not run a model.

    Why does the legacy Falcon chat require a larger deposit?

    The contract quotes a conservative maximum for all allowed work and fee caps, not an exact future network fee. At the recorded prices, the short chat’s deposit is around 17–21 ETH while local measured compensation was about 0.72 ETH. Funding a request does not guarantee completion. Unused escrow becomes credit on completion or cancellation, and you withdraw it with a separate transaction.

    Who executes and pays for continuation transactions?

    S0UL uses caller-funded transactions: this browser estimates the remaining work, then your wallet confirms each batch within the app’s accepted spending limit. Pause or closing the tab stops future wallet requests; submitted transactions still settle. Resume with the request ID and a fresh estimate. The legacy Falcon chat separately prepays executor escrow and uses session IDs.

    Are autonomous agent actions live?

    S0UL’s deployed model can request one approved action per generation: read your ETH or GH0ST balance, save or read theme/language/mode preferences, add/list/complete tasks, or wait. Its controller requires the exact approved action; mismatched output is rejected. A caller must fund and confirm the execution transactions. General-purpose autonomous agents remain research work.

    What will $GH0ST trading fees fund?

    The project commits 100% of $GH0ST trading fees it receives to further research toward a financially feasible on-chain LLM and agent. This is a project allocation policy, not a claim that every exchange fee is controlled by GH0ST or automatically routed by a verified fee contract.
